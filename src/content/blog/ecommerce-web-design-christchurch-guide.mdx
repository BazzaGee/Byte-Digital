## Why Ecommerce Is Different in Christchurch

Selling online from Christchurch has specific advantages and challenges. On the advantage side: New Zealand online spending keeps growing, local delivery is straightforward within the city, and "Christchurch" searches still carry buying intent — people searching for "online flower delivery Christchurch" want a local florist, not an Auckland warehouse.

Your website has to work harder: faster, clearer, and more trustworthy than the competition.

## Choosing Your Platform

There is no single "best" ecommerce platform — there is the best platform for your products, volume, and budget. The three paths we build most often:

### Shopify

Shopify is New Zealand's most popular hosted platform, and for good reason. It handles hosting, security, and payment compliance for you, integrates natively with NZ shipping and payments, and scales smoothly. Choose it when you want reliability and speed to launch over complete design control.

### WooCommerce

WooCommerce turns WordPress into a full store. It is open source, has no platform fees, and offers unmatched flexibility for custom product types, subscriptions, and content-led selling. Choose it when you already run WordPress or need deep customisation — our [WordPress development](/services/wordpress-development/) team builds these regularly.

### Headless Commerce

For maximum performance, a headless setup pairs a commerce engine with a blazing-fast front-end built on Astro or Next.js. It costs more upfront but delivers sub-second loads and superior SEO — which compounds into lower acquisition costs over time. For a deeper comparison of the underlying architecture, see our [headless CMS vs traditional CMS guide](/guides/headless-cms-vs-traditional/).

## Getting Payments Right: Windcave, Stripe, and the NZ Stack

Nothing kills a sale faster than a checkout that does not offer the payment method your customer expects. For New Zealand stores, that usually means offering a mix:

- **Stripe** — the default for most new stores: cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay with simple flat pricing
- **Windcave** — one of NZ's most established gateways (formerly Payment Express), strong with credit cards, internet banking, and POLi; common with established retailers
- **Afterpay and Zip** — buy-now-pay-later is increasingly expected, especially for fashion and homeware
- **Account2Account** — bank transfer payments that cut fees on high-value orders

If you are weighing one gateway against another, our breakdown of [Windcave vs Stripe for NZ stores](/blog/windcave-vs-stripe-nz-payment-gateways/) covers real fee structures, settlement times, and when each makes sense. Whichever you choose, the integration must be configured for NZD, GST, and NZ payment compliance from day one — retrofitting is expensive.

## Shipping That Does Not Eat Your Margin

Shipping configuration is where many first-time store owners lose money. Get it right before launch:

- NZ Post rate tables with weight and size calculations
- Rural delivery surcharges for addresses beyond main centres
- Free shipping thresholds that lift average order value
- Click and collect for Christchurch locals
- Clear international rules if you sell beyond NZ

## Design Decisions That Drive Conversion

A pretty store that does not convert is an expensive hobby. The design elements that consistently move the needle:

1. **Speed** — every extra second of load time costs sales. Mobile-first is non-negotiable: most NZ traffic is mobile.
2. **Frictionless checkout** — guest checkout, minimal form fields, progress indicators, and multiple payment methods.
3. **Trust signals** — real photos, reviews, physical address, and clear returns policy. Christchurch buyers check that you are local and real.
4. **Product pages that answer questions** — dimensions, materials, shipping times, and what is in the box, before the customer has to ask.
5. **Search and filtering** — customers who cannot find a product in three clicks leave.
