## Introduction

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is arguably the most powerful free marketing tool available to Christchurch businesses. When someone searches for "pool builder Christchurch" or "café near me," the Google Business Profile listings — also known as the local pack or map pack — appear prominently at the top of search results, often above traditional organic listings.

Businesses that optimise their GBP effectively see significantly more calls, website visits, and direction requests. At Byte Digital, we have helped Christchurch businesses transform their Google profile from an afterthought into their primary lead generation channel.

## Understanding How Google Business Profile Works

Before diving into optimisation tactics, it helps to understand how Google determines which businesses appear in local search results.

Google considers three primary factors:

- **Relevance**: How well does your business match what the searcher is looking for?
- **Distance**: How far is your business from the searcher's location?
- **Prominence**: How well-known and well-reviewed is your business?

You cannot control distance, but you can maximise relevance and prominence through deliberate optimisation.

## Complete Profile Setup

### Business Information

Every field in your profile sends a signal to Google about your business. Complete everything:

- **Business name**: Use your actual business name — do not keyword stuff (e.g., "Dave's Pools Christchurch" not "Christchurch Pool Builder Swimming Pool Construction Dave")
- **Primary category**: Choose the most specific category available. If you are a residential pool builder, select that rather than the generic "Swimming Pool Contractor"
- **Secondary categories**: Add all relevant categories. A plumber might add "Drainage Service," "Hot Water System Supplier," and "Gas Fitter"
- **Business description**: Write a detailed, natural description that includes your services, service area, and what makes you unique. Include Christchurch and relevant suburbs naturally
- **Services**: Add individual services with descriptions and pricing where appropriate
- **Service areas**: Define the specific Christchurch suburbs and Canterbury areas you serve
- **Hours of operation**: Be precise and update for holidays. If your hours vary by day, set each day individually
- **Attributes**: Enable all relevant attributes like "Women-led," "Free parking," "Wheelchair accessible," etc.

### Contact Information

- **Phone number**: Use a local Christchurch number (03 area code), not a 0800 number if possible — Google may associate local numbers more strongly with local results
- **Website**: Link to your website and ensure the URL is correct
- **Appointment URL**: If you take bookings online, add the direct booking link
- **Messaging**: Enable Google Messages to let customers text you directly from your profile

## Photo Strategy

Photos are one of the most underutilised aspects of Google Business Profile optimisation. Businesses with more photos receive significantly more engagement.

### Photo Types to Upload

- **Exterior photos**: Show your storefront, signage, or vehicle branding
- **Interior photos**: Give customers a feel for your physical space
- **Team photos**: People connect with people — show the faces behind your business
- **Work photos**: For service businesses, photos of completed projects are gold
- **Product photos**: For retail businesses, clear product images with descriptions
- **At-your-work photos**: Action shots showing your team in the field

### Photo Best Practices

- Upload new photos regularly — aim for at least 2-3 new photos per month
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 720x720px)
- Name your image files descriptively before uploading (e.g., "pool-construction-halswell-christchurch.jpg")
- Add geotagged photos when possible
- Rotate your cover photo seasonally to keep your profile fresh
- Include photos with your team in them — they receive more engagement than product-only shots

## Review Management Strategy

Reviews are one of the strongest ranking signals for your Google Business Profile. They also directly influence whether someone chooses your business over a competitor.

### Getting More Reviews

- Ask immediately after delivering a great experience — timing matters
- Send a follow-up email or text message with a direct link to your review form
- Create a simple QR code that links to your review page and display it in your shop or on your invoices
- Train your team to ask for reviews verbally
- Do not incentivise reviews — Google's guidelines prohibit offering rewards for reviews

### Responding to Reviews

- Respond to every single review, positive and negative
- Thank the reviewer by name and reference specific details from their review
- For negative reviews, apologise, acknowledge the issue, and offer to resolve it offline
- Keep responses professional and concise — avoid getting defensive
- Respond within 24-48 hours to show you are active and engaged

## Google Business Posts

Google Business Posts are like mini social media updates that appear directly on your profile. Many Christchurch businesses are not using this feature at all, which means you can stand out simply by posting regularly.

### Post Types

- **What's New**: Announce new services, products, or business updates
- **Offers**: Promote special deals or seasonal promotions
- **Events**: Promote upcoming events, workshops, or open days
- **Products**: Showcase specific products with photos, descriptions, and pricing

### Posting Best Practices

- Post at least once per week — consistency matters more than volume
- Include a compelling photo with every post
- Include a clear call-to-action with a button (Learn More, Book, Call, etc.)
- Keep text concise — 150-300 words is ideal
- Use posts to highlight Christchurch-specific content and local events

## Advanced Optimisation Tactics

### Q&A Section

The Questions and Answers section on your GBP is frequently overlooked. Pre-populate it with common questions and detailed answers:

- What areas in Christchurch do you service?
- What are your pricing and payment options?
- How quickly can you start a project?
- Do you provide free quotes?

### Insights and Analytics

Google provides detailed insights about how people interact with your profile. Monitor these regularly:

- **Search queries**: What keywords are people using to find you?
- **Customer actions**: Are they calling, visiting your website, or requesting directions?
- **Photo views**: Which photos get the most engagement?
- **Compared to similar businesses**: How does your engagement stack up against local competitors?

### Spammer Fighting

Report competing businesses that violate Google's guidelines:

- Keyword-stuffed business names
- Businesses operating from virtual offices or residential addresses when they should not be
- Businesses listing in areas they do not serve
- Duplicate listings
