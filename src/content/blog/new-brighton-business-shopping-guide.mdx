## Introduction

New Brighton has quietly become one of Christchurch's best days out — and one of its most interesting places to run a business. The pier, the redeveloped mall, the He Puna Taimoana hot pools, and a growing strip of cafés and shops have turned the seaside suburb into a genuine destination. This guide maps what is here, why locals keep coming back, and what the revitalisation means for the businesses trading along the coast.

## The Anchor: Brighton Mall and the Retail Core

Brighton Mall is the suburb's retail heart, mixing national chains with the independents that give New Brighton its character: discount stores, pharmacies, specialty food shops, and the kind of hands-on service retailers that malls-with-parking-lots have forgotten. The ongoing redevelopment has lifted foot traffic meaningfully — and the businesses inside are racing to keep up with customers who now expect to find them online as well as in person.

For retailers still running on word of mouth alone, the gap is real: shoppers plan trips to New Brighton from across the city, and they check what is there before they come. A shop that cannot be found in a "New Brighton shops" search simply misses that planning moment.

## The Beachfront: Cafés, Hot Pools and the Pier

The beachfront strip is New Brighton's postcard — and its busiest economic zone:

- **He Puna Taimoana hot pools** — the hot pools complex that put the suburb back on the map, drawing visitors year-round, not just in summer
- **The New Brighton Pier** — one of the longest ocean piers in the country, and the suburb's signature photo
- **Beachfront cafés and eateries** — from coffee-and-ice-cream stops to proper brunch destinations, with the indoor market and weekend events bringing steady crowds
- **The outdoor playground and skate park** — which means families, which means midweek trade as well as weekends

Hospitality businesses here live or die on being discovered twice: once by locals looking for "brunch near me" and once by visitors planning a beach day. Photos, menus, hours, and reviews online are the difference between a packed deck and a quiet Tuesday.

## Local Services: The Quiet Economy

Behind the retail front, New Brighton supports the full range of suburban services — hair and beauty, dentists and health providers, gyms, trades, vets, and professional services. Two things make the service economy here distinctive:

1. **A loyal residential base** — Seaview, South New Brighton, and the eastern suburbs treat New Brighton as their town centre, and they prefer local providers when they can find them
2. **Seasonal visitor surges** — summer crowds create demand spikes that well-prepared businesses capture online before anyone arrives at the beach

For service businesses, the practical takeaway: when a new resident searches "hairdresser New Brighton" or a visitor searches "cafe near New Brighton pier", the top three map results get the call. ## Planning Your Visit (or Your Move)

Practical notes for visitors: the mall and beachfront are an easy walk apart, parking is free and plentiful compared to the CBD, the libraries and community centre anchor the mall's west end, and weekend markets and events are listed on local community pages. Bring a jacket even in summer — it is still the coast.
