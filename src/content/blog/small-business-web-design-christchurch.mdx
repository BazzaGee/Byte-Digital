## Introduction

Most small business websites in Christchurch fail in one of two opposite directions: they are empty brochures that generate nothing, or they are overbuilt platforms with every feature imaginable and no visitors. This guide is the middle path — what a small business website actually needs to produce enquiries, and what you can safely skip.

## The Five Things That Must Be Right

### 1. Speed (or nothing else matters)

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone over mobile data. If your site takes more than ~3 seconds to show something useful, they are gone — and Google knows it, which is why speed is a ranking factor. This is the quiet advantage of modern builds over template stacks; our [web development approach](/services/web-development/) treats sub-second loads as a requirement, not a nice-to-have. Test your current site with our [free audit tool](/tools/free-website-audit/) and see the real numbers.

### 2. One obvious action per page

Small business sites convert when every page answers two questions: *what do you do* and *what do I do next*. A "Get a Quote" button above the fold beats a hero slider every single time. Pick your primary action — phone, form, booking — and make it unmissable.

### 3. Proof you are real

Christchurch customers check whether you are genuine before contacting you: real photos of your team or work, a physical service area, reviews, and a street-level Google map. Stock photography actively hurts small businesses — customers can smell it. A case study or two with real details converts better than any adjective; see how we structure proof in our [case studies](/case-studies/).

### 4. Mobile-first everything

Over 60% of local searches happen on phones, often while the customer is literally walking toward a purchase decision. Design for the phone first, desktop second. Test every page one-handed.

### 5. Being findable locally

A brilliant site with no visitors is a billboard in the desert. Small businesses win local search with three tools: a properly optimised [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-christchurch/), suburb-relevant pages, and [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) fundamentals done consistently. Budget for this from day one — it is not a phase-two project.

## The Expensive Extras You Can Skip

- **Bespoke illustrations and animations** — nice, rarely revenue-producing at small-business budgets
- **A blog nobody maintains** — better no blog than a blog whose newest post is three years old; if you do publish, our [content approach](/blog/web-design-trends-christchurch-2026/) shows how to make each post earn its keep
- **Every social feed embedded** — slows your site and sends visitors away
- **Custom functionality before demand exists** — build the booking system when bookings strain the phone line, not before

## What a Sensible Small Business Build Looks Like

For most Christchurch small businesses, the right scope is:

1. **Home page** — what you do, who for, proof, one clear action
2. **Service pages** — one per core service, written around what customers actually search
3. **About** — real people, real photos, why you do this
4. **Contact** — form, phone, hours, map, and your service area
5. **Location/suburb pages** — if you serve distinct areas, dedicated pages for each (this is the highest-ROI SEO asset most small businesses never build; we explain why in our [suburb targeting guide](/blog/christchurch-suburb-seo-targeting-guide/))

That is five to fifteen pages — enough to rank, convert, and maintain, without bloat.

## The Maintenance Truth

Websites decay: plugins break, content dates, Google changes its mind. Either budget a care plan ([ours start simple](/services/website-maintenance/)) or calendar a monthly hour to update, check Search Console, and refresh one page. An unmaintained site quietly slides down rankings while looking fine — until it suddenly is not fine.
