## What is the CookTwo app?

CookTwo is a **collaborative adaptive nutrition app for couples** — a live, production-grade Progressive Web App (PWA) that solves one of the most common friction points in any relationship: *what's for dinner tonight?*

Two partners, different goals. One trying to lose weight, the other trying to build muscle. But they still want to cook and eat together. CookTwo makes that possible by letting both partners share one kitchen in the app: a synced grocery list, a shared pantry, and AI-generated meals that plate two ways with portions calculated for each partner's body composition and dietary goals.

This is not a concept or a landing page. It is a fully functional, installable application — designed, engineered, and launched by Byte Digital, and available to use today at [cooktwo.com](https://cooktwo.com).

## Who is CookTwo for?

CookTwo is built for couples who:

- Have **different dietary goals** — weight loss vs. muscle building, vegan vs. omnivore, or any combination
- Want to **cook together** instead of preparing separate meals every night
- Are tired of the nightly "what should we make?" negotiation
- Want **AI-powered meal suggestions** based on what is already in their kitchen
- Need a **shared grocery list** that syncs in real time so nothing gets bought twice

The app supports omnivore, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, keto, paleo, and gluten-free diets, with hard allergen guardrails that block unsafe meals before they are ever shown.

## How CookTwo works

CookTwo is designed around a simple five-step flow that couples set up once and use daily:

1. **Create your kitchen** — enter your name, dietary preferences, body goals, and what you already have at home. Takes about two minutes.
2. **Link with your partner** — share a six-digit invite code. Your partner enters it. Instantly connected — one shared kitchen, two profiles.
3. **Stock your pantry** — type what you have in natural language ("chicken, 2 cups rice, spinach"). The AI parses, categorises, and remembers it.
4. **Shop together** — one shared grocery list with real-time sync under 100ms. AI auto-categorises items. One tap moves food to the pantry after a shop.
5. **Cook together** — follow one recipe. The AI gives each partner their own plating — different portions, different macros, same delicious dinner.

## The five core features

CookTwo ships with five features, all live and production-tested:

- **Adaptive Shared Cooking** — the signature feature. One recipe, two plates. The app calculates personalised portions and macro targets for each partner automatically using TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) and the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
- **Real-Time Shared Grocery List** — add milk at home, and it appears on your partner's phone at the store in under 100ms. Powered by WebSocket connections via Cloudflare Durable Objects, with offline queuing and automatic reconnection.
- **Pantry-First AI** — type ingredients in natural language and the AI parses, categorises, and stores them, then suggests meals you can make right now with what you already own. Reduces food waste and decision fatigue.
- **AI Meal Planning** — a conversational AI plans dinner from your pantry, or adds missing ingredients straight to your shared shopping list. Built on a multi-provider chain (DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, ZAI) with automatic fallback so it stays available even when a provider has an outage.
- **Real-Time Sync Architecture** — every change across grocery items, pantry, and meal plans syncs instantly between both partners, with offline support that reconciles cleanly on reconnect.

## The architecture

We architected CookTwo on a modern edge-first stack chosen specifically to deliver low-latency real-time collaboration at scale, without the operational overhead of traditional servers.

- **React 19** powers the front-end, using concurrent features to deliver a responsive, app-like experience that installs to the home screen as a PWA.
- **Cloudflare Workers** run the serverless back-end at the edge, in 300+ cities worldwide, with sub-millisecond cold starts.
- **Cloudflare D1** provides fast SQLite reads at the edge for pantry items, grocery lists, and meal plans.
- **Durable Objects** act as per-household WebSocket hubs, giving each couple a dedicated real-time room with sub-100ms sync and offline-friendly state.
- **A multi-provider AI chain** (DeepSeek, Alibaba, and ZAI) provides resilient meal generation and pantry intelligence, automatically failing over between providers.
- **Stripe** handles subscription billing per household, not per person — one payment covers both partners.

The result is an application that feels instant and native, runs on any device, and costs a fraction of a traditional server-based architecture to operate.
