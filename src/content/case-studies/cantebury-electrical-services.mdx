## The Challenge

Canterbury Electrical Services is a licensed electrical contractor serving residential and commercial clients across Christchurch and the wider Canterbury region. With a team of eight electricians, they handle everything from routine safety inspections and switchboard upgrades to full commercial fit-outs and emergency repairs. Despite their size and capability, their digital presence was stuck in the early 2010s.

Their existing website was a desktop-only Joomla installation that had not been updated since 2015. It loaded in over 9 seconds on mobile, had no secure HTTPS certificate, and displayed a "not secure" warning in Chrome that immediately eroded visitor trust. The contact method was limited to a single phone number and an email address — there was no online booking, no quote request form, and no after-hours option. When potential customers searched for "electrician Christchurch" on their phones, they were met with a tiny, illegible desktop layout that required horizontal scrolling just to find the phone number.

The business owner, Dave Henderson, was particularly frustrated by after-hours enquiries. Electrical emergencies don't follow business hours, and many potential customers were searching for an electrician at 7pm on a Saturday evening. Without any way to capture those leads electronically, they were going straight to competitors who had mobile-friendly websites with easy booking options. Dave estimated he was missing 20-30 after-hours call-outs per month during winter, the peak season for electrical faults.

## The Strategy

Our strategy was built around the reality that most people searching for an electrician are in an urgent situation. A blown circuit, a flickering light, or a tripped safety switch is not something people research leisurely — they need help now, and they need it from the device in their hand. Every element of the new website was designed with this urgency in mind.

We prioritised a mobile-first design approach, ensuring that the most important actions — calling, booking, and requesting a quote — were accessible within a single thumb tap on any screen size. We also implemented an automated after-hours booking system that could capture emergency enquiries outside business hours and notify the on-call electrician immediately via SMS.

The SEO strategy targeted both urgent transactional keywords ("emergency electrician Christchurch," "electrician near me") and higher-value planned keywords ("house rewiring Christchurch," "EV charger installation Canterbury"). We also created a Google Ads landing page optimised for emergency search terms with a click-to-call button as the primary conversion action.

## The Solution

The new website was built on Astro with a mobile-first responsive design that loaded in under 1 second on 4G connections. The homepage featured a prominent "Call Now" button that initiated a phone call on tap, a clean service menu covering all major electrical services, and an emergency banner at the top of the page directing urgent enquiries to a dedicated after-hours number.

The automated booking system was the centrepiece of the conversion strategy. It allowed customers to select their service type (emergency repair, scheduled maintenance, installation, inspection), choose a preferred time slot from real-time availability, enter their address and contact details, and submit the booking — all within 60 seconds on a mobile phone. After-hours bookings were flagged as urgent and triggered an immediate SMS notification to the on-call electrician with the customer's address, contact number, and a brief description of the issue.

Each service page was optimised for specific search queries with detailed content explaining the service, typical costs, turnaround times, and Canterbury-specific considerations like seismic compliance for switchboard upgrades and solar panel electrical requirements. We also built a dedicated emergency electrical services page that ranked for urgent search terms and featured a click-to-call button that was impossible to miss.

For SEO, we implemented a comprehensive local strategy including Google Business Profile optimisation, location-specific service pages for Christchurch suburbs, citation building across NZ trade directories, and a regular content publishing schedule targeting seasonal electrical concerns — heater safety checks in autumn, air conditioning wiring in spring, and Christmas lighting installation in November.

## The Results

The impact was immediate and sustained across both peak and off-peak seasons:

- **180% increase in service calls** — from 45 per month to 126+
- **After-hours bookings increased by 340%** — from 8 to 35+ per month
- **Mobile conversion rate of 12%** — compared to industry average of 3-4%
- **Ranked #1 for "electrician Christchurch"** within 8 weeks
- **Ranked page one for "emergency electrician Christchurch"** in 4 weeks
- **PageSpeed score of 97 on mobile** with a load time of 0.9 seconds
- **Google Business Profile grew from 12 to 200+ reviews** with a 4.9 rating
- **Annual revenue increased by an estimated $320,000** in the first year

Dave Henderson was candid about the transformation: "I used to think a website was just a digital business card. Now it's our number one sales channel. The after-hours booking system alone has probably generated $80,000 in emergency call-out revenue that we would have lost to competitors. Every time I get an SMS notification at 10pm with a new booking, I think about how much money we used to leave on the table."

## What Made the Difference

Understanding the urgency of electrical service searches was critical to this project's success. Most web designers would build a standard service page with a contact form at the bottom. We recognised that someone searching for an electrician at 9pm on a Saturday wants to talk to someone immediately, not fill out a form and wait for a response. The click-to-call button and SMS-powered after-hours booking system addressed this reality directly, capturing leads that no form-based approach could reach.

The seasonal content strategy also proved highly effective. By publishing targeted content about electrical safety during Canterbury's cold, damp winters and solar installation during summer, we maintained strong search visibility year-round rather than experiencing the feast-or-famine cycle that plagues most trade businesses in New Zealand.
