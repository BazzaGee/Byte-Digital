## What's Possible for Your Emergency Service Business

A licensed electrical contractor serving Christchurch had a team of eight electricians handling everything from routine inspections to emergency repairs. Despite their size and capability, their digital presence was stuck in the early 2010s. Their desktop-only website loaded in over 9 seconds on mobile, had no HTTPS certificate, and displayed a "not secure" warning.

The owner was particularly frustrated by after-hours enquiries. Electrical emergencies don't follow business hours, and many potential customers were searching at 7pm on a Saturday evening. Without any way to capture those leads electronically, they were going straight to competitors.

## The Approach We Would Take

Most people searching for emergency services are in an urgent situation. They need help now, and they need it from the device in their hand. Our approach:

1. **Mobile-first design** ensuring calling, booking, and quoting are accessible within a single tap
2. **An automated after-hours booking system** that captures emergency enquiries and notifies your team via SMS
3. **SEO targeting both urgent and planned keywords** to capture demand year-round
4. **Click-to-call functionality** that's impossible to miss on any device

## What You Could Achieve

- **180%+ increase in service calls** — from 45 per month to 126+
- **340%+ increase in after-hours bookings** — from 8 to 35+ per month
- **12%+ mobile conversion rate** — compared to industry average of 3-4%
- **Page-one Google rankings** within 8 weeks for your main keywords
- **PageSpeed score of 97+** on mobile with load times under 1 second
- **Google Business Profile growing** to 200+ reviews with 4.9+ rating
- **Significant annual revenue increase** from captured after-hours demand

## What Makes the Difference

Understanding the urgency of emergency service searches is critical. Someone searching for your service at 9pm on a Saturday wants to talk to someone immediately, not fill out a form and wait. Click-to-call buttons and SMS-powered after-hours booking systems capture leads that no form-based approach could reach.

Seasonal content strategy also proves highly effective. By publishing targeted content about your services during peak demand periods, you maintain strong search visibility year-round rather than experiencing the feast-or-famine cycle that plagues most trade businesses.
