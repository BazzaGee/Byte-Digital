## The Challenge

CleanPro Pool Services is a family-owned pool maintenance and renovation company serving residential and commercial clients across greater Christchurch. They offered a comprehensive range of services including regular maintenance, equipment repairs, pool resurfacing, and new pool installations. The business had strong word-of-mouth and a loyal customer base, but their digital presence was virtually non-existent.

Their old website was a single-page WordPress site built in 2016 that looked like it belonged in a museum. It featured a low-resolution stock photo of a generic pool, a phone number, and an email address. There was no information about services, no pricing, no testimonials, and certainly no way for potential customers to get a quote without calling during business hours. The site ranked nowhere on Google for any relevant search terms, and their Google Business Profile was unclaimed.

Owner James Walker estimated that he was losing at least 15-20 potential customers per month because they couldn't find him online or couldn't be bothered calling during work hours to get a simple price estimate. In a seasonal industry where demand spikes dramatically between October and March, every missed lead during peak season represented significant lost revenue.

## The Strategy

We identified that the biggest barrier to conversion for pool service businesses is the quoting process. Most pool owners want a quick, no-commitment price estimate before they engage with a company. The traditional approach — call, leave a message, wait for a callback, schedule a site visit, wait for a written quote — can take days and creates friction that kills conversion.

Our strategy focused on building an instant quote calculator that could provide accurate pricing estimates based on pool size, service type, and frequency. This would be the centrepiece of a new website designed to capture leads 24/7 without requiring any human intervention. Combined with local SEO targeting Christchurch pool-related searches and Google Maps integration, the goal was to make CleanPro the most visible and easiest-to-engage pool service company in Canterbury.

## The Solution

The new CleanPro website was built on Astro with a clean, modern design that communicated professionalism and reliability. The homepage featured real photos of CleanPro's work, customer testimonials, and a prominent call to action directing visitors to the instant quote calculator.

The quote calculator was the centrepiece of the site. Built as an interactive multi-step tool, it asked users for their pool type (concrete, fibreglass, vinyl), approximate size, desired service frequency (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or one-off), and any additional requirements. Based on these inputs, the calculator generated an instant price range with a clear breakdown. Users could then submit their details to receive a formal quote via email, with 80% of the form pre-filled from their calculator inputs.

We implemented a comprehensive local SEO campaign targeting keywords like "pool cleaning Christchurch," "pool maintenance Canterbury," "pool renovation Christchurch," and "pool equipment repair." Location-specific landing pages were created for key service areas including Christchurch CBD, Riccarton, Bryndwr, Fendalton, and Halswell. Each page featured genuine service photos from work completed in those areas.

Google Maps integration was embedded throughout the site, showing CleanPro's service coverage area and making it easy for customers to understand availability in their specific location. The Google Business Profile was claimed, optimised, and connected to an automated review request system that sent follow-up emails to customers three days after service completion.

## The Results

The results were dramatic from the very first month after launch, and continued to compound throughout peak season:

- **900% increase in quote requests** — from an average of 5 per month to 50+
- **Instant quote calculator used by 73% of website visitors**
- **Quote-to-customer conversion rate of 42%** — well above the industry average of 15%
- **Ranked #1 for "pool cleaning Christchurch" within 6 weeks**
- **Page one rankings for 22 pool-related keywords**
- **Google Business Profile grew from 0 to 120+ reviews** with a 4.8 average rating
- **Revenue increased by 180%** in the first year compared to the previous year

James Walker described the transformation: "Before this website, I was the quote guy. I'd spend hours every week driving to properties, measuring pools, writing quotes, and following up. Now the website does 90% of that work for me. I get a notification on my phone with a qualified lead, and most of the time the job is already sold before I even pick up the phone."

## What Made the Difference

The instant quote calculator was the game-changer. In the pool services industry, pricing transparency is extremely rare, and most competitors deliberately obscure their pricing to force phone contact. By being the first pool service company in Christchurch to offer instant, transparent pricing, CleanPro immediately differentiated themselves and captured a massive share of price-sensitive searchers who would have otherwise bounced from competitor sites.

The Google Maps integration and review generation system created a powerful local visibility flywheel. As more reviews accumulated, local search rankings improved, driving more traffic, generating more reviews, and further strengthening rankings. Within six months, CleanPro had become the most reviewed and highest-rated pool service company in Christchurch, creating a competitive moat that would be very difficult for rivals to overcome.
