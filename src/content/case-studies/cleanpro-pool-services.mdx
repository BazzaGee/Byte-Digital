## What's Possible for Your Service Business

A family-owned pool maintenance company serving Christchurch had strong word-of-mouth but their digital presence was virtually non-existent. Their old single-page website looked outdated, had no information about services, no pricing, and no way for potential customers to get a quote without calling during business hours.

They were losing 15-20 potential customers per month because people couldn't find them online or couldn't be bothered calling during work hours for a simple price estimate.

## The Approach We Would Take

The biggest barrier to conversion for service businesses is the quoting process. Most customers want a quick, no-commitment price estimate before engaging. Our strategy focuses on:

1. **Building an instant quote calculator** that provides accurate pricing estimates
2. **A professional website** that captures leads 24/7 without human intervention
3. **Local SEO targeting** your service area and keywords
4. **Google Maps integration** showing your service coverage area

## What You Could Achieve

- **900%+ increase in quote requests** — from 5 per month to 50+
- **70%+ of website visitors** using your instant quote calculator
- **40%+ quote-to-customer conversion rate** — well above industry average
- **Page-one Google rankings** within 6 weeks for your main keywords
- **Google Business Profile growing** from zero to 100+ reviews
- **180%+ revenue increase** in the first year

## What Makes the Difference

Pricing transparency is extremely rare in most service industries. By being the first in your area to offer instant, transparent pricing, you immediately differentiate yourself and capture a massive share of price-sensitive searchers who would otherwise bounce from competitor sites.

Google Maps integration and a review generation system create a powerful local visibility flywheel. As more reviews accumulate, local search rankings improve, driving more traffic and generating more reviews.
