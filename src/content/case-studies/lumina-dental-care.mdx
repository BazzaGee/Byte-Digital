## What's Possible for Your Healthcare Practice

A well-established dental practice in Christchurch had a reputation for excellence but their online presence was actively costing them new patients. Their template-based website took over 6 seconds to load, featured stock photos, and provided no real information about their services. They were invisible on Google for their highest-value services.

Their practice manager was spending over 10 hours every week managing phone calls for appointments, answering the same questions repeatedly, and manually entering patient details. Sound familiar?

## The Approach We Would Take

Our approach is built around understanding the patient journey in healthcare. Patients research treatments, evaluate credibility, check pricing and availability, then book. Every element of our strategy smooths each step:

1. **A custom-built website** that communicates clinical expertise and trust
2. **An integrated booking system** that eliminates drop-off and saves admin time
3. **A targeted SEO campaign** for high-value treatments
4. **An automated patient communication system** that frees up your front desk

## What You Could Achieve

- **Page-one Google rankings** for your most valuable keywords
- **10+ hours per week saved** on appointment management and patient admin
- **400%+ increase in online bookings** — from 15 per month to 78+
- **165%+ increase in new patient enquiries**
- **Website load times under 1 second**
- **PageSpeed scores of 100** on both mobile and desktop

## What Makes the Difference

The integration between your website and your practice management software is the single biggest factor in success. By removing the friction of redirecting patients to a third-party booking system, you can convert 400%+ more website visitors into actual appointments.

Comprehensive, transparent content that addresses patient concerns — including pricing, which most practices avoid — positions you as the most trustworthy option in your market.
