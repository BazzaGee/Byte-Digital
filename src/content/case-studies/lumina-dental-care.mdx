## The Challenge

Lumina Dental Care is a well-established dental practice in the Christchurch CBD with a team of four dentists, two hygienists, and a reputation for excellence in cosmetic and implant dentistry. Despite their clinical expertise and a loyal patient base, their online presence was a significant weak point that was actively costing them new patients.

Their existing website was a template-based WordPress site that had been neglected for years. It took over 6 seconds to load, featured stock photos of generic smiling patients, and provided no real information about their services beyond basic bullet points. The online booking system was a third-party widget that redirected patients to an external site, resulting in a 65% drop-off rate. Most concerning, they were invisible on Google for their highest-value services — searches like "dental implants Christchurch" and "cosmetic dentistry Christchurch" were dominated by competitors with inferior clinical outcomes but stronger digital marketing.

The practice manager, Sarah Chen, was spending over 10 hours every week managing phone calls for appointments, answering the same questions about services and pricing repeatedly, and manually entering patient details into their practice management software. The team knew they needed a better system, but they had been burned by two previous web developers who delivered overpriced, underperforming websites.

## The Strategy

Our approach was built around a clear understanding of the patient journey in dental healthcare. We identified that patients researching dental services go through a predictable process: they search for the specific treatment they need, evaluate the clinic's credibility and expertise, check pricing and availability, and then book an appointment. Every element of our strategy was designed to smooth each step of this journey.

We proposed a complete digital transformation covering four areas: a custom-built website that communicated clinical expertise and trust, an integrated booking system that eliminated drop-off, a targeted SEO campaign for high-value treatments, and an automated patient communication system that would free up the front desk.

## The Solution

We rebuilt the Lumina Dental Care website from the ground up using Astro, creating a fast, accessible, and visually refined design that reflected the premium nature of their services. The homepage featured a clean layout with clear calls to action, genuine team photos, and patient testimonials. Each treatment page — dental implants, teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign, and general dentistry — was crafted as a comprehensive resource with detailed information about procedures, expected outcomes, recovery times, and transparent pricing ranges.

The online booking system was rebuilt as a native integration with their existing practice management software. Patients could now view real-time availability, select their preferred dentist, and complete the entire booking process without ever leaving the website. We also implemented a smart pre-appointment questionnaire that collected medical history and consent forms digitally, eliminating paper-based admin.

For SEO, we conducted extensive keyword research specific to the Christchurch dental market and created a content hub targeting 40+ treatment-specific keywords. Each service page was optimised with schema markup for medical practices, FAQ sections addressing common patient concerns, and internal linking strategies that guided visitors toward high-value treatments. The Google Business Profile was completely overhauled with professional photos, regular posts, and a review generation system.

## The Results

The transformation delivered results across every metric within four months:

- **Ranked #1 for "dental implants Christchurch"** — their most valuable keyword
- **Ranked page one for 28 of 40 targeted keywords**
- **10 hours per week saved** on appointment management and patient admin
- **Online bookings increased by 420%** — from 15 per month to 78+
- **New patient enquiries increased by 165%**
- **Website load time reduced from 6.1s to 0.8s**
- **PageSpeed score of 100 on both mobile and desktop**

Practice manager Sarah Chen described the impact: "Before, I spent my entire morning on the phone managing bookings and answering questions. Now patients book themselves online, and our website answers 90% of their questions before they even walk through the door. I've been able to focus on patient experience instead of admin. The ROI on this project has been extraordinary."

## What Made the Difference

The integration between the website and their practice management software was the single biggest factor in this project's success. By removing the friction of redirecting patients to a third-party booking system, we converted 420% more website visitors into actual appointments. Combined with the SEO strategy that drove targeted traffic for high-value treatments, the practice saw a meaningful increase not just in patient volume but in revenue per patient, as more visitors were discovering their cosmetic and implant services.

The content strategy also played a crucial role. Dental patients are anxious and need detailed, trustworthy information before committing to treatment. By creating comprehensive, transparent content that addressed their concerns — including pricing, which most dental practices avoid publishing — Lumina Dental Care positioned itself as the most trustworthy and transparent option in the Christchurch market.
