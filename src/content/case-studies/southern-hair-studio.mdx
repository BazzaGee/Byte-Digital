## What's Possible for Your Creative Business

A premium hair salon in Christchurch's Merivale district had built a strong Instagram following of over 8,000 followers. Despite this social media success, their website was doing almost nothing to convert that attention into actual bookings. The only way to book was to call during business hours or send a DM on Instagram.

The owner was spending over two hours every day managing Instagram DMs from potential clients asking about availability, pricing, and stylist specialties. Many conversations never resulted in bookings because response time was too slow.

## The Approach We Would Take

Your social media following is an untapped goldmine of potential bookings. Our strategy focuses on three interconnected elements:

1. **A visually stunning website** that serves as a digital portfolio
2. **An integrated online booking system** connected to your management software
3. **A seamless social media-to-website conversion funnel** that turns engagement into booked appointments

## What You Could Achieve

- **300%+ increase in online bookings** — from 40 per month to 160+
- **8%+ social media-to-booking conversion rate** from link clicks
- **DM management time reduced** from 2 hours per day to 15 minutes
- **Page-one Google rankings** within 5 weeks for your area
- **PageSpeed score of 98+** with load times under 1 second
- **Google Business Profile growing** to 200+ reviews with 4.9+ rating
- **35%+ increase in revenue per staff member** due to better booking utilisation

## What Makes the Difference

In creative industries, visual inspiration is the primary driver of booking decisions. Clients see a result they want and then need to figure out how to get it. By eliminating every step between inspiration and booking, we convert a significant percentage of casual social media browsers into confirmed appointments.

A gallery that grows with your work creates a self-reinforcing marketing machine — more impressive work attracts more visitors, generating more bookings, producing more satisfied clients, and leading to more portfolio-worthy results.
