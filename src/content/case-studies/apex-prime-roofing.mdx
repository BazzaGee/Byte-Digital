## The Challenge

Apex Prime Roofing had been serving Christchurch homeowners for over 15 years but their website was holding them back. Built on an outdated WordPress theme from 2014, the site was slow (8+ second load times), not mobile-friendly, and ranked on page 3 of Google for their most important keyword: "roofing Christchurch."

Despite being one of the most experienced and reputable roofing companies in Canterbury, they were consistently losing jobs to newer competitors with sharper online presence. Their existing website had broken links, poor navigation, and zero lead capture mechanisms beyond a generic contact form buried on the fifth page of a confusing menu structure. The phone still rang occasionally from word-of-mouth referrals, but management knew they were leaving tens of thousands of dollars on the table every month by neglecting their digital presence.

Google Analytics showed an average session duration of just 45 seconds and a bounce rate exceeding 78%. Mobile users, who made up 65% of their traffic, were met with a desktop layout that required constant pinching and zooming. The site had no structured data, no local SEO strategy, and no content marketing plan. In short, it was invisible to search engines and frustrating for human visitors.

## The Strategy

We started with a comprehensive digital audit that covered technical SEO, competitor analysis, user experience, and content strategy. The audit revealed several critical issues: over 40 broken links, no HTTPS redirect, uncompressed images totalling 12MB on the homepage alone, and zero schema markup. Meanwhile, their top three competitors all had modern websites ranking on page one with clear conversion paths.

Our strategy centred on three pillars: rebuild the website on a modern stack for maximum speed, implement a rigorous local SEO campaign targeting Christchurch and Canterbury, and create a conversion-optimised user journey with multiple touchpoints for lead capture. We chose Astro as the framework for its exceptional out-of-the-box performance and built a custom design that projected the quality and reliability Apex Prime Roofing was known for in the real world.

## The Solution

The rebuild began with a complete information architecture overhaul. We simplified the navigation from seven confusing menu levels to a clean four-page structure: Home, Services, About, and Contact. Each service page was crafted around a specific search intent, targeting keywords like "roof repairs Christchurch," "new roof installation Canterbury," and "roof inspection Christchurch."

We designed the site mobile-first, recognising that most homeowners searching for roofing services are on their phones, often standing in their driveway looking at a damaged roof. The new design featured a prominent "Get a Free Quote" button fixed to the bottom of every screen on mobile, large tap-friendly phone numbers, and a multi-step quote form that felt effortless to complete.

Performance was a top priority. By leveraging Astro's static site generation, we eliminated server-side rendering overhead. Images were served in WebP format with lazy loading, CSS was minified and critical-path optimised, and JavaScript was reduced to under 50KB total. The result was a page load time of 1.2 seconds on a 3G connection.

For local SEO, we claimed and optimised their Google Business Profile, built citations across 50+ New Zealand directories, created location-specific landing pages for Christchurch, Rangiora, and Ashburton, and implemented comprehensive schema markup including LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ structured data. We also launched a monthly blog targeting long-tail roofing queries specific to the Canterbury region.

## The Results

Within three months of launching the new website and SEO campaign, the results exceeded every target we had set:

- **215% increase in monthly inbound leads** — from an average of 8 leads per month to 25+
- **Page load time reduced from 8.2s to 1.2s** — a 6x improvement
- **PageSpeed score improved from 34 to 98** on mobile
- **Ranked #1 for "roofing Christchurch"** within 90 days, up from page 3
- **Average time on site increased from 45 seconds to 3+ minutes**
- **Bounce rate dropped from 78% to 32%**
- **Google Business Profile reviews doubled** from 40 to 80+ with a 4.9 average rating

The impact on Apex Prime Roofing's bottom line was transformative. Owner Mark Thompson reported that the business had to hire two additional roofing crews to keep up with demand. "We used to chase work during the slow season," Mark shared. "Now the phone rings every day, and we're booking out three months in advance. The website has completely changed how we operate."

## What Made the Difference

The key differentiator in this project was the combination of technical excellence with deep local market understanding. We didn't just build a fast website — we built a digital lead generation engine specifically calibrated for the Christchurch roofing market. Every design decision, every keyword targeted, and every conversion element was informed by data about how Canterbury homeowners search for and choose roofing contractors.

The multi-step quote form alone was responsible for a 340% increase in form submissions compared to the old single-field contact form. By asking the right questions at the right time — roof type, approximate area, urgency level — we filtered out tyre-kickers and delivered Apex Prime Roofing with high-quality, sales-ready leads.
